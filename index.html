05 - About

Fashion designer exploring culture, identity, and future narratives.

I am Rhythm, a B.Des Fashion student at Chandigarh University with a deep interest in fashion, cinema, music, and visual storytelling. My work explores the intersection of culture, identity, speculative systems, and emotional design.

Alongside fashion design, I work across editorial visuals, research-driven concepts, cinematic worldbuilding, and experimental media. My creative practice is influenced heavily by film language, music culture, and contemporary visual aesthetics.

Previously involved in modeling and creative direction, I approach design not only as functionality, but as emotion, identity, and experience.
